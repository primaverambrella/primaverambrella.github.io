Plans

  • Free: 15 imports and 3 exports per calendar month, with every feature. No payment needed.
  • Pro: unlimited imports and exports, sold as a subscription, $5 a month or $29 a year. The first 7 days after installing the plugin are a free Pro trial, no card or payment needed; after that, every payment has a 14-day money-back guarantee. Current prices are on the pricing page.

We may change prices for future billing periods. If the price of your subscription changes, we email you before the next charge, and you can cancel before it happens.

Payments

  • Refunds follow our refund policy.
