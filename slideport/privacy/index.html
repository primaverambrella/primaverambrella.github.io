Slideport privacy policy

Short version: your designs and files stay with you. The plugin sends only licence checks and anonymous usage events you can turn off.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who we are

Slideport is a Figma plugin made by Primavera Studio, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here. In this policy “we” means Primavera Studio. This policy covers the plugin, the Slideport server and this website. Questions: open an issue or write to .

What stays on your computer

  • Your Figma document. The plugin reads selected frames only to build an export file, and that file is created on your computer. Nothing from your Figma file is uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Files you drop into the plugin: PowerPoint, Word and PDF. They are parsed inside the plugin window and never uploaded.
  • Your settings: language, defaults, font substitutions. They are stored in Figma's plugin storage on your device.

Anonymous usage statistics

To find bugs and see which features are used, the plugin sends anonymous events to PostHog (US region):

  • the plugin opened, which screen was shown, which button was clicked;
  • for imports and exports: file type and size, number of layers or slides, time taken, export mode, number of missing fonts;
  • error messages, so we can fix them.

Events carry a random installation ID stored in Figma's plugin storage. They contain no design content, texts, file names, page addresses, Figma user names or emails.

Turn it off: Settings → Anonymous statistics. After that no events are sent.

Feedback form

If you send feedback from the plugin settings, your message and the optional email you type go to the same PostHog project so we can read and answer it. Leave the email empty to stay anonymous.

Payments

Pro is sold through lava.top, a platform for selling digital products. lava.top collects your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy; we never see your card. In our lava.top account we can see your order: email, amount, card mask, plan and subscription status. We use them only to turn on Pro, answer billing questions and handle refunds.

Right after checkout, the pricing page asks the Slideport server on Cloudflare for your personal license key; the key is calculated from your email and is not stored anywhere. When you enter your email and key in Settings → Slideport Pro, the plugin sends them to the same server, which checks the key and asks lava.top whether that email has an active subscription. The server keeps no copy of either. To avoid asking lava.top on every launch, the answer (whether Pro is active and until when) is cached on Cloudflare for up to 10 minutes under a one-way hash of your email. The plugin stores your email and key on your device and re-checks the subscription every few days.

Email updates

If you leave your email in a form on this website, we store it on the Slideport server with the date and which form you used. We use it only to tell you when Slideport is available and about major updates, and never share or sell it. To be removed, write to us and we delete it.

Free plan and trial

The free plan allows a number of imports and exports per month, and the 7-day Pro trial is given once. So that these limits can't be reset by switching computers or clearing plugin data, the plugin sends the Slideport server your Figma user ID, the random installation ID described above and its local counts. The server checks them against its own clock and stores only one-way salted hashes:

  • of your Figma user ID and installation ID, with the date the plugin was first opened, the trial end date and the number of imports and exports per month;
  • of your network (your IP address without its last part), for 30 days, to limit how many trials one network can start.

It never receives your name, email, files or page addresses. Pro users are not counted. Without a connection the plugin allows a few actions and syncs later; with no connection at all the free plan can't be checked.

When you check Pro, the plugin also sends your Figma user ID, so one subscription can be used in up to three Figma accounts within 30 days. The server keeps a salted hash of the purchase email and of each account with the last check date.

Technical data

  • IP addresses. The Slideport server uses your IP address for a moment to limit the number of requests per minute and stop abuse. The address itself is not stored; only a salted hash of the network part is kept for 30 days, as described above.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address and browser details under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address under Google's privacy policy. When you click Get Pro, you go to the lava.top payment page, which may set its own cookies to process payments and prevent fraud. The site has no ad trackers. Analytics runs only if you allow it — see below.

Website analytics

If you click Allow in the banner, this website loads Microsoft Clarity to show us how visitors use it: pages viewed, where visitors came from, country, device and browser, clicks and scrolling, and anonymised recordings of the visit. Text you type into fields is masked and never recorded. Clarity sets its own cookies for this. It is not used on the plugin, and it is not used for ads.

If you click No thanks or ignore the banner, nothing is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with Analytics settings at the bottom of every page. Clarity processes data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement and keeps it for up to 13 months.

Where data is processed and for how long

  • Usage events and feedback are stored by PostHog in the United States and kept for up to 12 months.
  • The Slideport server runs on Cloudflare's global network. It stores nothing except the free plan, trial and Pro account hashes and the short caches described above.
  • Order records are kept by lava.top for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

We process this data to provide the plugin and the Pro subscription you asked for, and, for anonymous statistics and security limits, in our legitimate interest in keeping Slideport working; you can switch statistics off at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us what data about you we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use. Write from the email in question and we will answer within 30 days. For payment data, you can also contact lava.top support directly. If you live in the EU, the UK or another country with data-protection law, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

Children, changes, contact

Slideport is a professional design tool and is not aimed at children. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or requests to delete feedback you sent: open an issue or email .
