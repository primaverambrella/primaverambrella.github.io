Slideport privacy policy

Short version: your designs and files stay with you. The plugin sends a page address only when you import by URL, and anonymous usage events you can turn off.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who we are

Slideport is a Figma plugin made by Primavera Studio, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here. In this policy “we” means Primavera Studio. This policy covers the plugin, the Slideport server and this website. Questions: open an issue or write to .

What stays on your computer

  • Your Figma document. The plugin reads selected frames only to build an export file, and that file is created on your computer. Nothing from your Figma file is uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Files you drop into the plugin: HTML, ZIP, MHTML, EML, PowerPoint and PDF. They are parsed inside the plugin window and never uploaded.
  • Your settings: language, defaults, font substitutions, proxy address. They are stored in Figma's plugin storage on your device.

Importing by URL

When you paste a web address and click Load, the address is sent to the Slideport proxy, a small service on Cloudflare Workers. The proxy downloads that public page, its images, fonts and stylesheets, and passes them back to the plugin. Sites built with JavaScript frameworks are opened in a headless browser on Cloudflare first.

  • The proxy does not store the pages it fetches and keeps no logs of addresses in its own code. Responses may stay in Cloudflare's cache for up to 5 minutes.
  • Cloudflare, as the hosting provider, processes requests under its own privacy policy.
  • You can set your own proxy in Settings → Proxy for URLs; then requests never reach ours.
  • If you don't import by URL, the proxy is never contacted.

Anonymous usage statistics

To find bugs and see which features are used, the plugin sends anonymous events to PostHog (US region):

  • the plugin opened, which screen was shown, which button was clicked;
  • for imports and exports: file type and size, number of layers or slides, time taken, export mode, number of missing fonts;
  • error messages, so we can fix them.

Events carry a random installation ID stored in Figma's plugin storage. They contain no design content, texts, file names, page addresses, Figma user names or emails.

Turn it off: Settings → Anonymous statistics. After that no events are sent.

Feedback form

If you send feedback from the plugin settings, your message and the optional email you type go to the same PostHog project so we can read and answer it. Leave the email empty to stay anonymous.

Payments

Pro is sold through Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card. In our Paddle account we can see your order: email, name, country, plan, amounts and subscription status. We use them only to turn on Pro, answer billing questions and handle refunds.

Right after checkout, the pricing page asks the Slideport server on Cloudflare for your personal license key; the key is calculated from your email and is not stored anywhere. When you enter your email and key in Settings → Slideport Pro, the plugin sends them to the same server, which checks the key and asks Paddle whether that email has an active subscription. The server keeps no copy of either. To avoid asking Paddle on every launch, the answer (whether Pro is active and until when) is cached on Cloudflare for up to 10 minutes under a one-way hash of your email. The plugin stores your email and key on your device and re-checks the subscription every few days.

Technical data

  • IP addresses. The Slideport server uses your IP address for a moment to limit the number of requests per minute and stop abuse. It is not stored.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address and browser details under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address under Google's privacy policy. The pricing page loads Paddle's checkout, which may set its own cookies to process payments and prevent fraud. The site itself has no analytics, no ad trackers and no cookies of its own.

Where data is processed and for how long

  • Usage events and feedback are stored by PostHog in the United States and kept for up to 12 months.
  • The Slideport server runs on Cloudflare's global network. It stores nothing except the short caches described above.
  • Order records are kept by Paddle for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

We process this data to provide the plugin and the Pro subscription you asked for, and, for anonymous statistics and security limits, in our legitimate interest in keeping Slideport working; you can switch statistics off at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us what data about you we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use. Write from the email in question and we will answer within 30 days. For payment data, you can also contact Paddle directly. If you live in the EU, the UK or another country with data-protection law, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

Children, changes, contact

Slideport is a professional design tool and is not aimed at children. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or requests to delete feedback you sent: open an issue or email .
