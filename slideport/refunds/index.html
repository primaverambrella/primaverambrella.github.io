Slideport refund policy

If Pro isn’t right for you, you get your money back. No forms, no arguing.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any Slideport Pro payment: the first one and every renewal, monthly or yearly. You don’t need to give a reason.

Try first. The first 7 days after installing Slideport are free Pro, no card. Every payment after that comes with this 14-day guarantee.

How to get a refund

  1. Write to or open an issue with the email you used at checkout. We refund from our lava.top account, no forms.

lava.top returns the money to the original payment method. It usually arrives within 10 business days, depending on your bank.

After 14 days

Later refunds are possible if Slideport didn’t work for you because of a bug we couldn’t fix, or if you were charged by mistake. Write to us and we’ll sort it out. When a subscription is cancelled without a refund, Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights as a consumer, it doesn’t replace them. lava.top’s user terms also apply.
