Slideport refund policy

If Pro isn’t right for you, you get your money back. No forms, no arguing.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of any Slideport Pro payment: the first one after the trial and every renewal, monthly or yearly. You don’t need to give a reason.

Free trial first. Pro starts with a 7-day trial. Cancel during the trial and you are never charged.

How to get a refund

  1. Find the receipt email from Paddle and use the link in it, or look up your order at paddle.net and request a refund there.
  2. Or write to , or open an issue with the email you used at checkout. We’ll ask Paddle to refund you.

Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for our orders and returns the money to the original payment method. It usually arrives within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

After 14 days

Later refunds are possible if Slideport didn’t work for you because of a bug we couldn’t fix, or if you were charged by mistake. Write to us and we’ll sort it out. When a subscription is cancelled without a refund, Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights as a consumer, it doesn’t replace them. Paddle’s buyer terms also apply.
