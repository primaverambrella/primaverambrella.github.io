How buying works

  1. Click Start free trial and pay in the Paddle checkout window: card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay, depending on your country.
  2. Right after payment this page shows your personal license key. Open Slideport → Settings → Slideport Pro and enter the same email and the key. Pro turns on right away, on every computer where you use Slideport.
  3. You won't be charged during the 7-day trial. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing.

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders: it takes the payment, charges the right tax, and sends the receipt and invoice.
