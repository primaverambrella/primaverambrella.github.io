How buying works

  1. Enter your email, click Get Pro and pay on the lava.top payment page: card, Apple Pay or PayPal.
  2. You come back here, and this page shows your personal license key. Open Slideport → Settings → Slideport Pro and enter the same email and the key. Pro turns on right away, on every computer where you use Slideport.
  3. The first 7 days after installing are free Pro, no card. Bought and changed your mind? Ask for a refund within 14 days of any payment, no questions asked.

Payments are processed by lava.top, a platform for selling digital products. It takes the payment, sends the receipt and handles your card details; we never see them.
